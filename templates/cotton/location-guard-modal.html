{# Heading & Explanation #}

Location Access Required

FieldTrack requires device location to verify attendance and duty presence. Please ensure location is enabled on your device and allowed in your browser.

{# Insecure Origin Notice (Shown automatically if accessed via HTTP LAN IP) #}
Local Wi-Fi Network Note

Mobile browsers restrict high-precision GPS over unencrypted HTTP (192.168.x.x). You can tap "Continue to Dashboard" to proceed with network verification.

{# Quick Step Guide #}

Quick Steps:

  • 1 Ensure Location / GPS is turned ON in your device quick settings.
  • 2 Tap the lock / settings icon in your browser address bar and set Location to Allow.
  • 3 Tap Verify Location below.
