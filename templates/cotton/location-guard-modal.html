Device GPS / Location Required

FieldTrack requires active GPS location to verify attendance. You cannot check in, check out, or use the staff system until location is enabled.

{# Step-by-Step Instructions Card #}

How to enable / যেভাবে চালু করবেন:

  • 1 Open your device quick settings / drop-down menu and turn ON Location / GPS.
  • 2 In your browser address bar, tap the lock/settings icon and set Location to Allow.
  • 3 Click the blue button below to detect and verify your location.
