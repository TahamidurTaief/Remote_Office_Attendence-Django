How it works

A random Fernet MEK is generated and wrapped with a KEK derived from your server's SECRET_KEY. The raw key is never stored. Downloads are decrypted on-the-fly — files on disk are always encrypted.

⚠ Rotating the key will make existing encrypted backups unreadable without the old SECRET_KEY.
